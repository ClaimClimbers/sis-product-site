How it works

Every step, in the open.

A hearing-ready doctor letter is a sequence. You should be able to point to each stage. Specialists prepare. A licensed doctor reviews and signs, or says no. No mystery queue. No release without a licensed doctor.

01

Tell us the need

You tell us what the hearing needs: a doctor’s letter about work limits, a physical work-limits letter, a mental work-limits letter, or a records screen (MSS, physical RFC, mental MRFC, or screen). We take only what is required to open the file. Upload only for now. No fax or clinic system bridge yet.

02

Upload medical records

You upload medical records. SAMPLE details only on this site. Production packets wait on a privacy policy. Each record is listed so the file is easy to check.

03

Build the packet

We hash the packet and check for gaps. Missing pieces are named before anyone drafts an opinion. You get, in writing, what the hearing file still lacks.

04

Draft with citations

Records specialists draft the letter language against the record list. Citations stay attached to the file. Specialists build. They do not sign.

05

Licensed doctor review

A licensed MD or DO is the only clinical gate and signs, or declines. If the record cannot support the opinion, the packet is declined. Ready for review is not the same as released.

06

Delivery

You receive the signed packet as a PDF with the record list. We do not file the claim. We do not appear at the hearing unless that work is scoped later (NEED INPUT).

07

Audit trail

Hashes, gap report, draft history, and the doctor’s decision stay with the packet. Helpers exist so the file can be explained. No invented credentials in the trail.

What you get

  • A supportability decision before a full opinion.
  • Cited draft language a licensed MD or DO can accept, edit, or refuse.
  • A gap list you can work before the hearing week.

What we do not do

  • Guarantee approval or a faster decision.
  • Charge a percent of benefits.
  • Talk about a different benefits system. This is SSDI.
  • Release a packet without licensed MD/DO attestation.

Integrity first

We only move an opinion forward when the record can carry it. If the file is thin, we decline. That is the product. Not a refund slogan. Not an outcome promise.
