How it works

Every step, in the open.

A hearing-ready medical opinion packet is a sequence. You should be able to point to each stage. No mystery queue. No unsupervised release.

01

Intake

You tell us the hearing need: MSS, physical RFC, mental MRFC, or a supportability screen. We take only what is required to open the file. Upload-only in the beachhead. No fax or EHR bridge yet.

02

Upload records

You upload medical records. SAMPLE PHI only on this site. Production packets wait on a PHI policy. Each exhibit is listed so the file is inspectable.

03

Packet assemble

The packet is hashed and gap-checked. Missing domains are named before anyone drafts an opinion. This is the HALLEX-style chore: what the hearing file still lacks, in writing.

04

Draft with citations

Packet specialists draft MSS, RFC, or MRFC language against the exhibit list. Citations stay attached to the record. Specialists assemble. They do not attest. Reserved-issue language stays out.

05

MD/DO gate

A licensed MD or DO is the sole clinical gate and attests, or declines. If the record cannot support the opinion, the packet is declined. Packet pass is not product release.

06

Delivery

You receive the attested packet as a PDF with the exhibit index. We do not file the claim. We do not appear at the hearing unless that work is scoped later. NEED INPUT.

07

Audit trail

Hashes, gap report, draft history, and the attestation decision stay with the packet. Disclosure helpers exist so the file can be explained. No invented credentials in the trail.

What you get

  • A supportability decision before a full opinion.
  • Cited draft language a licensed MD or DO can accept, edit, or refuse.
  • A gap list you can work before the hearing week.

What we do not do

  • Guarantee approval or a faster decision.
  • Charge a percent of benefits.
  • Lead with VA nexus language. This is SSD.
  • Release a packet without licensed MD/DO attestation.

Stand-by integrity

We only move an opinion forward when the record can carry it. If the file is thin, we decline. That is the product. Not a refund slogan. Not an outcome promise.
