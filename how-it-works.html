01

Tell us what you need

You tell us whether you need a doctor’s letter about work limits, a physical work-limits letter, a mental work-limits letter, or a records screen. We take only what is required to open the file. Upload only for now. No fax or clinic system bridge yet.

02

Send medical records

You send medical records. SAMPLE details only on this site. Production packets wait on a privacy policy. Each record is listed so the file is easy to check.

03

We build the packet

We list every record and name what is still missing before anyone drafts a letter. You get that list in writing. We do not write a doctor’s letter over holes in the file.

04

Draft the letter from the records

Records specialists draft the letter language against the record list. Every line stays tied to the file. Specialists prepare. They do not sign.

05

A licensed doctor reviews and signs

A licensed doctor (MD or DO) is the only person who reviews and signs. If the records are not enough, we say no. Ready for review is not the same as a letter going out.

06

You get a clear next step

You receive the signed letter as a PDF with the record list, or a written no. We do not file the claim. We do not appear at the hearing unless that work is scoped later (NEED INPUT).

07

The file stays explainable

The record list, the missing-pieces list, the draft history, and the doctor’s decision stay with the packet. Helpers exist so the file can be explained. No invented credentials in that history.

What you get

  • A clear yes or no before a full letter.
  • Draft language a licensed doctor can accept, edit, or refuse.
  • A written list of what the file still lacks, so you know the next step.

What we do not do

  • Guarantee approval or a faster decision.
  • Charge a percent of benefits.
  • Talk about a different benefits system. This is SSDI.
  • Send a letter without a licensed doctor’s signature.

We say no if the records are not enough

We only move a letter forward when the records can carry it. If the file is thin, we say no. That is the product. Not a refund slogan. Not an outcome promise.
