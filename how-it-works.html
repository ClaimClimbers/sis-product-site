What we do not do

  • Guarantee approval or a faster decision.
  • Charge a percent of benefits.
  • Talk about a different benefits system. This is SSDI.
  • Sell a letter SKU as the Home lead until letters ship.

Integrity first

We only move a path forward when the record can carry it. If the file is thin, we decline. That is the product. Not a refund slogan. Not an outcome promise.
