Clinical gate

Licensed MD or DO. Sole clinical gate.

The clinical gate is attestation by a licensed MD or DO. Records specialists assemble packets and draft language from the exhibits. The MD or DO attests, or declines. If the record cannot support the opinion, we decline.

Prepare

Packet specialists assemble

Records specialists assemble packets, hashes, gap reports, and cited draft language from the exhibits. They stop at the gate. They do not sign. They do not invent NPI, license, or diagnosis.

Attest

MD or DO attests

A licensed MD or DO is the sole clinical gate. They review the record and the draft. They accept, edit, or refuse. That decision is the only release. There is no universal approval card.

Decline

We stop

Unsupported domains stay unsupported. CASE-001 is the fixture fail path. SAMPLE PHI only. A decline is integrity, not a failed sale.

What we will not invent

No diagnoses. No NPI. No board credentials. No clinical opinions except from a licensed MD or DO. No stock doctor photographs that imply a named clinician. SAMPLE language only on this site.

Release rule

  • Packet PASS is not product release.
  • MD/DO attestation is required to export.
  • Fixture drafts stay labeled SAMPLE.

Safety rule

  • Refuse to invent missing medicine.
  • Keep reserved-issue language out of drafts.
  • Keep production PHI off this public site.
