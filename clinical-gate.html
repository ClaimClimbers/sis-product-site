When we say no

We never invent a diagnosis.

The product is a medical record review. Records specialists read your file and explain it in everyday words. If the records are not enough, we say so. Then you get a clear next step.

Records specialists assemble what is in the file, what is still missing, and a plain-language explanation. They do not invent a diagnosis or a clinical opinion.

Explain

Everyday words

Decline

We say no

If part of the file has no support in the records, that part stays open or we say no. Honesty is the product working, not a failed sale.

What we will not invent

No diagnoses. No license numbers. No board credentials. No stock photos that imply a named treating clinician. SAMPLE language only on this site.

Honesty rule

  • The review names what the file can and cannot support.
  • Demo copy stays labeled SAMPLE.
  • We never invent missing medicine.

Safety rule

  • Refuse to invent missing medicine.
  • Keep guessed language out of the review.
  • Keep live medical records off this public site.
