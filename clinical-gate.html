Doctor review

A licensed doctor reviews and signs.

Specialists prepare the packet from your medical records. Only a licensed doctor (MD or DO) reviews it and signs. We say no if the records are not enough. Then you get a clear next step. We never invent a diagnosis so a letter can go out.

Request a packet See the steps

Prepare

Specialists build the packet

Records specialists assemble the packet, the record list, what is still missing, and draft language from the medical records. They stop there. They do not sign. They do not invent a license, a diagnosis, or a clinical opinion.

Sign

A licensed doctor signs

A licensed doctor (MD or DO) is the only person who reviews and signs. They review the records and the draft. They accept, edit, or refuse. That decision is the only release. There is no unsupervised sign-off.

Decline

We say no

If a part of the letter has no support in the records, that part stays open or we say no. SAMPLE language only. A paid screen that refuses is the product working, not a failed sale.

What we will not invent

No diagnoses. No license numbers. No board credentials. No clinical opinions except from a licensed MD or DO. No stock doctor photographs that imply a named clinician. SAMPLE language only on this site.

Release rule

  • A packet ready for review is not a released product.
  • A licensed doctor (MD or DO) must sign before a letter goes out.
  • Demo drafts stay labeled SAMPLE.

Safety rule

  • Refuse to invent missing medicine.
  • Keep guessed language out of drafts.
  • Keep live medical records off this public site.
