Care first, then the file

You are carrying someone else's records.

That is a real job. A record review helps you understand the file. It is not legal advice, and it is not a promise. If they already have a representative, this can still sit beside that help.

What to gather

Dated medical records that belong to the person applying. Visit notes, imaging, therapy notes, medication lists when they exist. On this public site, do not paste private live records into a demo. When you are ready to send real files, we will use a proper private path.

What you will understand afterward

What the file shows. What is still missing. A next step you can take together. We do not file the Social Security claim for you.

$395 lifetime record review. Or $129.99 a year for the app.

No cut of benefits. No promise about the decision. No doctor letters for sale here.

What families usually send

Where you are in the process. The medical records you can find. Anything important we should know. No invented facts.

What you get back

A plain-language explanation. A list of gaps. Or a clear written no if the file cannot carry the path.

Honesty is care

If the records are not enough, we say so. That protects the person you love.

Bring the file you actually have.

We will talk through the review and the gaps, not a pitch about winning.
