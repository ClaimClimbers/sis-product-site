Care first, then the records

You are carrying someone else’s medical records.

That is a real job. This product is the doctor’s letter about work limits the hearing can use. It is not legal advice, not a promise, and not a stand-in for a representative if one already exists.

What to gather

Dated medical records that belong to the person applying. Visit notes, imaging, therapy notes, medication lists when they exist. SAMPLE details only on this public site. Do not paste live records into this demo. Production packets wait on a privacy policy.

How the letter helps

We name what the file still lacks, in writing, before the week of hearing. Draft language stays tied to the records. If a licensed doctor reviews and signs, you get a PDF the hearing can use. We do not file the SSDI claim.

You are not the doctor

Specialists build the packet list and draft language. They stop there. A licensed doctor (MD or DO) reviews and signs, edits, or says no. There is no letter without that review.
