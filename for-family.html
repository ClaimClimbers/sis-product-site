Empathy, then the file

You are carrying someone else’s records.

That is a real job. This product is the medical-opinion packet the hearing can exhibit. It is not legal advice, not a promise, and not a substitute for the representative if one already exists.

What to upload

Dated medical records that belong to the claimant. Visit notes, imaging reports, therapy notes, medication lists when they exist. SAMPLE PHI only on this public site. Production packets wait on a PHI policy. Do not paste live records into this demo.

How the packet helps the hearing

A gap report names what the file still lacks, in writing, before the week of hearing. Cited draft language stays attached to the exhibits. If a licensed MD or DO attests, you receive a PDF the file can exhibit. We do not file the SSDI claim.

You are not the doctor

Packet specialists assemble hashes, the exhibit list, and draft language. They stop at the gate. A licensed MD or DO reviews the record and attests, edits, or refuses. There is no unsupervised release.
