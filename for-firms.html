For firms

An operator desk for disability firms.

You already know the sentence. You need an MSS or RFC from a doctor. This product is that packet, prepared for the hearing, gated by an MD or DO, with a weekly gap check you can run as chore rather than as spectacle.

Desk

Prepare. Do not substitute counsel.

We do not file the claim. We do not replace the representative. We do not guarantee the ALJ outcome. We deliver a supportability-gated packet the firm can exhibit.

What firms send

  • Hearing need: MSS, physical RFC, mental MRFC, or screen.
  • Medical records, upload-only in the beachhead.
  • Context the clinician should see. No invented facts.

What firms get back

  • Gap report and exhibit index.
  • Cited draft, or a documented decline.
  • MD/DO attested PDF when release is allowed.
