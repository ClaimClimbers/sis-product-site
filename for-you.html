Plain language

What these letters mean.

MSS · Medical Source Statement

A doctor-signed written opinion about what you can still do. The hearing file can exhibit it. It is not a promise you will be approved.

Physical RFC

Sit, stand, walk, lift, carry, and breaks — only when dated clinical records can support numbers. If a domain has no support, that domain stays open or the packet is declined. SiS list $450.

Mental MRFC

Mental residual functional capacity for an SSDI hearing. Same rule as physical: cited language from the exhibits, then a licensed MD or DO attests. SiS list $300.

Supportability screen · $149

A records review first. If the file cannot carry an opinion, we stop. A paid screen that refuses is the product working, not a failed sale.

The clinical gate

  • A licensed MD or DO is the only person who attests.
  • Packet specialists assemble. They do not sign.
  • If the record cannot support the opinion, we decline.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee of SSDI approval.
  • Not a percent of your benefits.
  • Not a consultative exam or IME.
  • Not a VA nexus product.
