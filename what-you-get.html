What you get

The packet, named plainly.

Buyer language is MSS, physical RFC, and mental MRFC. The hearing needs a doctor-signed functional opinion. Packet specialists assemble that packet from the exhibits. We do not invent the medicine.

MSS

Medical Source Statement

A treating-style functional opinion the file can exhibit. Companion to RFC when the record supports both. Packet specialists draft cited language from the exhibits. Released only after licensed MD/DO attestation.

Physical RFC

Physical residual functional capacity

Sit, stand, walk, lift, carry, and breaks when the dated clinical record can support numbers. If a domain has no support, that domain stays open or the packet is declined.

Mental MRFC

Mental residual functional capacity

Mental MRFC for SSD hearings. Same gate as physical: cited draft from the exhibits, licensed MD/DO attestation, decline when unsupported. SAMPLE PHI language only on this site. No invented clinician.

Screen

Supportability screen

Records review first. CASE-001 is the fixture fail path: physical RFC marked FAIL / NEEDS_RECORDS on unsupported domains. SAMPLE PHI only. The decline is the product working.

Gap chore

Packet-gap check

HALLEX-style pre-hearing chore. Exhibit list, hashes, and a written gap report so counsel can see what the file still lacks before the week of hearing.

In the packet

  • Exhibit index with hashes.
  • Gap report.
  • Cited draft.
  • MD/DO decision to attest or decline.
  • PDF delivery and audit trail.

Not in the packet

  • A consultative exam or IME product.
  • VA nexus, DBQ, or C&P language.
  • Invented diagnoses, NPI, or credentials.
  • A promise the claim will be approved.
  • Percent of past-due benefits as a fee.

CASE-001 and CASE-002 are fixtures. SAMPLE PHI only. Packet PASS is not release. MD/DO gate still required.
