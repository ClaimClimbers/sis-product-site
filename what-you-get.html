What you get

The packet, named plainly.

The beachhead is a medical record review. Doctor letters about work limits come later. We do not invent the medicine. Plain names first: records review now; doctor’s letter, physical work-limits letter, mental work-limits letter (MSS, physical RFC, mental MRFC) on the waitlist.

Now

Medical record review

Records review first. If the file cannot carry an opinion later, we stop. SAMPLE language only. A paid review that refuses is the product working. SiS list $99.

Gap check

Packet-gap check

A written list of what the file still lacks. Record list and a missing-pieces report the firm or family can work.

Coming later

Doctor’s letter about what you can still do (MSS)

Waitlist. A doctor-signed written opinion the hearing file can use. Often paired with a physical work-limits letter when the file supports both. Released only after a licensed MD or DO signs.

Coming later

Physical work-limits letter (physical RFC)

Waitlist. Sit, stand, walk, lift, carry, and breaks when dated records can support numbers. If a part of the file has no support, that part stays open or the packet is declined.

Coming later

Mental work-limits letter (mental MRFC)

Waitlist. How mental limits affect work for an SSDI hearing. Same gate as physical: draft tied to the file, licensed MD or DO signs, decline when unsupported. SAMPLE language only on this site.

In the packet

  • Record list with hashes.
  • Gap report.
  • Cited draft.
  • MD or DO decision to sign or decline.
  • PDF delivery and audit trail.

Not in the packet

  • A consultative exam or independent medical exam.
  • Copy for a different benefits system. This is SSDI.
  • Invented diagnoses, license numbers, or credentials.
  • A promise the claim will be approved.
  • Percent of past-due benefits as a fee.

SAMPLE language only. A packet ready for review is not a released product. Licensed MD or DO review is still required when letters ship.
