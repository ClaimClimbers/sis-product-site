What a medical record review is (and is not)

Is

A careful read of your dated medical records, explained in everyday words. A written sense of what the file shows and what is still missing.

Is not

A doctor letter. We do not sell letters on this site. A consultative exam or a claim filing service. A promise about the decision.

What you send

Dated medical records that belong to you (or to the person you are helping). Visit notes, imaging reports, therapy notes, medication lists when they exist. SAMPLE details only on this public site. Do not paste live private records into a demo form. Production uploads wait on a privacy policy.

What you get back

A plain-English explanation of what the file shows. A list of what is still missing. A clear next step you can follow. An honest no when the records are not enough.

When we say no

If the records cannot carry a fair path forward, we say so. That is honesty. We never invent a diagnosis so a review can look complete.
