Straight answers in plain English.

What is this product?

A full medical record review for Social Security disability. Records specialists read your file and name, in plain words, what it can and cannot support. Not a consultative exam. Not an independent medical exam.

Who reviews the file?

Records specialists do the review. They explain the file in everyday words. They do not invent diagnoses.

What if the records are not enough?

We say no. A paid review that stops is the product working. SAMPLE language only. We will not invent a diagnosis.

What is the next step?

Start a record review. We walk that step with you. We do not promise Social Security will approve the claim.

I am the person applying. Where do I start?

Use For you. Start with a medical record review. Lead price is $395 lifetime. The yearly app is the quieter alternate on Pricing.

Can a family member start this?

Yes. Use For family. You can help a parent, spouse, or sibling gather medical records. Specialists build from the file. You are not the doctor. SAMPLE details only on this public site.

What are the prices?

Two public prices. Full medical record review $395 lifetime. Or the app for $129.99 a year. We do not charge a percent of benefits.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Social Security decides. We do not sell speed, win rate, or a rating. We sell a records review with honest gaps, or a documented no.

Is this only for Social Security disability?

Yes. This site is Social Security disability only. Security in Social is the only brand on these pages.

Will you use real claimant records on this site?

No. SAMPLE language only. Production packets wait on a privacy policy. Do not upload live records into this public demo.

Do you have a public domain or inbox yet?

Domain and legal entity are deferred. This site does not invent a web address or a clinical email. Use the contact form stub to copy a request.

Will you email my family for me?

No. We help you understand the records. You stay in charge of what gets sent.
