Ask the next step For you
What is this product?

A doctor’s letter about what you can and cannot do at work, built from your medical records for an SSDI hearing. Records specialists prepare the packet. A licensed doctor reviews and signs. We say no if the records are not enough. Not a consultative exam. Not an independent medical exam.

What is a doctor’s letter about work limits?

It is a signed written opinion about what you can still do at work, and what you cannot. There is also a physical work-limits letter and a mental work-limits letter. Firms may later call these MSS, physical RFC, and mental MRFC. Public pages lead with the plain names.

Who reviews and signs?

A licensed doctor (MD or DO). Records specialists build the packet from your medical records. They do not sign. Nothing goes out unsigned.

What if the records are not enough?

We say no. A paid screen that stops is the product working. SAMPLE language only. We will not invent a diagnosis so a letter can ship.

What is the next step?

Contact us. Tell us whether you need a records screen or a letter. We walk that step with you. We do not promise Social Security will approve the claim.

I am the person applying. Where do I start?

Use For you. The letters are named in plain words. SiS list is $149 / $300 / $450. A licensed doctor still reviews and signs.

Can a family member start this?

Yes. Use For family. You can help a parent, spouse, or sibling gather medical records. Specialists build from the file. You are not the doctor. SAMPLE details only on this public site.

What are the prices?

SiS list: screen $149, mental work-limits letter $300, physical work-limits letter $450. That is 75% of cited industry upfront. Named cites stay on Pricing. We do not charge a percent of benefits. Doctor sign-off fee is NEED INPUT if billed separate.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Social Security decides. We do not sell speed, win rate, or a rating. We sell a supportable packet or a documented no.

Is this only for Social Security disability?

Yes. This site is SSDI only. Security in Social is the only brand on these pages.

Will you use real claimant records on this site?

No. SAMPLE language only. Production packets wait on a privacy policy. Do not upload live records into this public demo.
