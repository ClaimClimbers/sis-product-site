What is this product?

A medical record review for Social Security disability. Records specialists read your file and name, in plain words, what it can and cannot support. Doctor-signed letters about work limits (MSS, physical RFC, mental MRFC) are coming later. Not a consultative exam. Not an independent medical exam.

What is a doctor’s letter about work limits?

Coming later. It is a signed written opinion about what you can still do at work, and what you cannot. There is also a physical work-limits letter and a mental work-limits letter. Firms may later call these MSS, physical RFC, and mental MRFC. Until they ship, join the waitlist. Public pages lead with the records review.

Who reviews and signs?

Records specialists do the review now. They do not sign. When doctor letters ship, a licensed doctor (MD or DO) is the only person who reviews and signs. Nothing goes out unsigned.

What if the records are not enough?

We say no. A paid review that stops is the product working. SAMPLE language only. We will not invent a diagnosis so a letter can ship later.

What is the next step?

Start a records review. If you want doctor letters when they ship, join the letter waitlist. We walk that step with you. We do not promise Social Security will approve the claim.

I am the person applying. Where do I start?

Use For you. Start with a medical record review. SiS list lead is $149. Letter prices ($300 / $450) are waitlist until they ship.

Can a family member start this?

Yes. Use For family. You can help a parent, spouse, or sibling gather medical records. Specialists build from the file. You are not the doctor. SAMPLE details only on this public site.

What are the prices?

SiS list lead: medical record review $149. Mental work-limits letter $300 and physical work-limits letter $450 are coming later / waitlist. That is 75% of cited industry upfront. Named cites stay on Pricing. We do not charge a percent of benefits. Doctor sign-off fee is NEED INPUT if billed separate.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Social Security decides. We do not sell speed, win rate, or a rating. We sell a records review with honest gaps, or a documented no.

Is this only for Social Security disability?

Yes. This site is SSDI only. Security in Social is the only brand on these pages.

Will you use real claimant records on this site?

No. SAMPLE language only. Production packets wait on a privacy policy. Do not upload live records into this public demo.
