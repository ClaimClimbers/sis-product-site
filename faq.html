FAQ

Straight answers.

If a question would require inventing a domain, a doctor, a price, or an outcome, the answer is NEED INPUT or no.

What is this product?

Hearing-ready SSD medical opinion packets. Medical Source Statements, physical RFC, and mental MRFC. Supportability-gated. Delivered for disability firms to exhibit. Not a consultative exam. Not an IME.

Why not lead with “IMO”?

That shorthand bleeds into VA language. Buyer-facing copy stays MSS, RFC, MRFC, and hearing-ready medical opinions. Internal shorthand is not the homepage.

Who attests?

An MD or DO. Bots prepare packets and drafts. Humans attest. There is no unsupervised release and no universal approval card.

What if the records cannot support an opinion?

We decline. CASE-001 is the fixture fail path: physical RFC marked FAIL / NEEDS_RECORDS. SAMPLE PHI only. A paid screen that stops is the product working.

What are the prices?

SiS list: screen $149, mental MRFC $300, physical RFC $450. That is 75% of cited industry upfront ($199 Dr. Kaako, $400 Essential Veteran Services, $600 Dr. Kaako). We do not charge a percent of benefits. Clinician attestation fee is NEED INPUT if billed separate.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. SSA decides. We do not sell speed, win rate, or a rating. We sell a supportable packet or a documented decline.

Is this a VA nexus product?

No. This site is SSD only. No nexus, DBQ, or C&P copy. Security in Social is the only brand on these pages.

Will you use real claimant records on this site?

No. SAMPLE PHI and fixtures only. Production packets wait on a PHI policy. Do not upload live records into this public demo.

Do you have a public domain or inbox yet?

Domain and legal entity are deferred. This site does not invent a web address or a clinical email. Use the contact form stub to copy a request.
