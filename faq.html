FAQ

Straight answers.

We are with you on the file, not on a win-rate promise. If a question would require inventing a domain, a doctor, a price, or an outcome, the answer is NEED INPUT or no.

Ask the next step For you
What is this product?

Doctor-signed letters about what you can and cannot do at work for SSDI hearings. A doctor’s letter about work limits, a physical work-limits letter, and a mental work-limits letter (MSS, physical RFC, mental MRFC). We only move forward when the records support it. Not a consultative exam. Not an independent medical exam.

Why not lead with industry shorthand?

Public pages lead with plain English. MSS, RFC, and MRFC appear in parentheses or on Pricing and For firms. Internal shorthand is not the homepage.

Will you stay with the file?

Yes. Specialists prepare from the medical file. A licensed MD or DO signs, or declines. We walk the next step with you. We do not promise Social Security will approve the claim.

Who signs?

A licensed MD or DO. Records specialists build the packet and draft language from the file. The doctor is the only clinical gate and signs, or declines. Nothing goes out unsigned.

I am the person applying. Where do I start?

Use For you. The letters are named in plain words. SiS list is $149 / $300 / $450. A licensed MD or DO still signs. We do not guarantee SSDI approval.

Can a family member start this?

Yes. Use For family. You can help a parent, spouse, or sibling gather records. Specialists build from the file. You are not the doctor. SAMPLE details only on this public site.

What if the records cannot support an opinion?

We decline. A paid screen that stops is the product working. SAMPLE language only. We will not invent a diagnosis so a letter can ship.

What are the prices?

SiS list: screen $149, mental work-limits letter $300, physical work-limits letter $450. That is 75% of cited industry upfront. Named cites stay on Pricing. We do not charge a percent of benefits. Clinician sign-off fee is NEED INPUT if billed separate.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Social Security decides. We do not sell speed, win rate, or a rating. We sell a supportable packet or a documented decline.

Is this only for Social Security disability?

Yes. This site is SSDI only. Security in Social is the only brand on these pages.

Will you use real claimant records on this site?

No. SAMPLE language only. Production packets wait on a privacy policy. Do not upload live records into this public demo.

Do you have a public domain or inbox yet?

Domain and legal entity are deferred. This site does not invent a web address or a clinical email. Use the contact form stub to copy a request.
